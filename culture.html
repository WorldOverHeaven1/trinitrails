Culture and festivals

A calendar of the biggest celebrations, and the music behind them.

Festival calendar

  1. February or March

    Carnival

    Two days of costume bands, calypso, soca and steelband music across the island.

  2. April

    Easter and Bake Season

    Kite flying, beach lime and plenty of freshly made bake.

  3. May to June

    Indian Arrival Day

    Cultural programs, music and dance honouring Indian heritage.

  4. August

    Emancipation Day and Independence

    Celebrations of freedom and national pride with parades and concerts.

  5. October or November

    Divali

    Rows of lit deyas, food and music for the festival of lights.

  6. December

    Parang Season

    Spanish-influenced Christmas music with cuatro and maracas.
